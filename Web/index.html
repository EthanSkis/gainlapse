Why it exists

The mirror lies.
The timelapse doesn't.

You see yourself every day, so you never see the change. Gainlapse takes one lined-up photo a week and stacks them until the progress is impossible to miss.

01 / Weekly photo

Every shot lines up

A guided camera with a timer and a faint overlay of last week's photo, so every picture matches the one before it. Pick your day and get a reminder.

02 / AI check-in

Honest, not harsh

After each photo, get short feedback on what's visibly changing and on your consistency, plus one thing to focus on this week. It never scores your body, guesses body fat or talks calories.

03 / Automatic timelapse

It builds itself

Every photo is added to your timelapse. Scrub through it, compare any two weeks, and export a video when you're ready to show it off.
