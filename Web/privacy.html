Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Gainlapse is published by Brian Travis Gardner. This policy explains what happens to your data. Questions: gainlapse@gmail.com.

No account, no ads, no tracking

Gainlapse has no sign-up. It doesn't show ads, doesn't include analytics or advertising tools, doesn't track you across apps and websites, and doesn't sell your data.

Photos on your phone

Your photos, check-ins and settings are saved inside the app on your iPhone, not in your photo library. Like other app data, they can be included in your iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on.

AI check-ins

Check-ins are optional, and the app asks before the first one. When they're on, each check-in sends this week's photo, usually one earlier photo, your goal, the pose and the week number to our server, hosted by Supabase in the United States, and on to Anthropic, whose Claude AI writes the feedback.

Our server handles photos in memory for that request only and doesn't save or log them. Anthropic processes them as our service provider under its commercial terms and privacy policy. Under those terms it doesn't train its models on this data by default, and it may keep data for a limited time.

You can turn check-ins off at any time in Settings. Your photos then stay on your phone.

Subscription checks

Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details. To confirm you're subscribed, the app sends Apple's signed record of your subscription with each check-in. That record includes the plan and subscription dates. To stop abuse, our server stores a scrambled (hashed) version of your subscription ID and the time of each check-in, and deletes them after about 30 days.

Sharing

A photo or video leaves Gainlapse in any other way only if you export it and choose where it goes.

Deleting your data

Delete all photos in the app removes every photo and check-in from the app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone, though existing backups may keep a copy until they're replaced. To ask about server records, email us.

Adults only

Gainlapse is for people 18 and older. Please don't use it if you're younger. If you think a minor has used our check-ins, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
