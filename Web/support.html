Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email gainlapse@gmail.com.

Cancel or manage your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. In Gainlapse, open Profile, tap the gear, then Manage subscription. You can also go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

On the paywall tap Restore, or in Settings tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

New phone

Photos are saved inside the app on your phone. They move to a new phone only through an iPhone backup or transfer, so keep a backup if you want to keep them.

Delete your photos

In Settings tap Delete all photos. This removes every photo and check-in from the app.
